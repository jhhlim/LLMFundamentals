### Slide 1 — Hook (talk to VCs)
“2–4 hours in Word/Canva per brochure. At $75–$150/hr, that’s **$150–$600 of labor per listing** — before a designer.  
We compress that to **minutes**: paste Compass URL → branded template → edit gaps → PDF.  
**Investor line:** turn marketing labor into productized margin.”

### Slide 2 — Demo
Show the Space. Narrate: “This is the $150–$600 turning into a workflow.”

### Slide 3 — Economics + expand
“Example: 8 listings/mo × 3 hrs × $100/hr ≈ **$2,400/mo** unlocked per agent — teams multiply.  
Next: LangSmith to debug failed imports (protect the save), then LLM polish, MLS, seller links.”

### Slide 4 — Close
“Stop paying hours for every flyer. Input the URL. Edit the template. **Keep the margin.**”
